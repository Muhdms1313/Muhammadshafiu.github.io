ABOUT ME

Who I Am

My name is Muhammad Shafiu. I was born in Nigeria, Kano State, specifically in Madobi Local Government Area. I currently live in Madobi.

I started my primary education in 2008 at madobi Iya special Primary School, Madobi, and completed it in 2014. I attended GTC Tiga for junior secondary school and Ibrahim Ayagi Dawakin Tofa Science College for senior secondary school.

In 2023, I proceeded to Kano State Polytechnic, Innovation and Vocational Enterprise Institute (IVEI), Matan Fada, Kano State, where I studied Software Engineering. I completed my programme in 2025 and obtained a National Innovation Diploma (NID).

Beyond Technology

I am also actively involved in tailoring and fashion design. This business has supported me financially and given me valuable practical experience while I was studying.

My experience in Kano city and at home in Madobi has helped me develop communication skills, confidence, business awareness, and the ability to interact with people.
